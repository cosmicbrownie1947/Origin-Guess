About Origin Guess!

What is Origin Guess!?

Origin Guess! is an interactive web-based game designed to teach people about AI by testing them on whether an image or text presented is AI or real. As artificial intelligence continues to expand everyday, it is becoming increasingly more difficult to tell what is authentic and what is not. Origin Guess! challenges users to critically think about what they see online and in their day to day life.

The Goal

The goal of Origin Guess! is to build up a generation that can detect AI and avoid it when they choose to. Building a sustainable future first starts with not buying into AI generated content, products, and scams. By detecting AI, Origin Guess! hopes to build a more authentic future for everyone.

Target Audience

Origin Guess! is primarily targeted to students and everyday internet users who want to better understand AI. Players continue through ten rounds, and at the end the game displays a final score based on their answers. In future versions of the game, a leaderboard will allow players to compete with friends and track high scores.

What Makes Origin Guess! Different?

Origin Guess! is designed to be accessible, engaging, and easy to understand. While other platforms exist that discuss AI-generated media, many are either overly technical or not interactive. Origin Guess! focuses on creating a simple and engaging experience that allows users to learn through gameplay.

The platform also avoids political or adult content so it can be used by younger audiences without introducing unnecessary bias or sensitive material. By combining education with gameplay, Origin Guess! encourages users to become more aware of the images they consume in everyday life and to question whether what they are seeing is actually real.

Why Origin Guess!?

Origin Guess! was created after Nichole grew frustrated with her grandfather frequently sending AI-generated images and talking about how “cool” and “stunning” they were. This sparked the idea to create a project that helps people recognize AI-generated content and understand how easily it can spread online.

Privacy Policy

Origin Guess! values user privacy and safety. We do not collect personal data beyond optional usernames used for gameplay features such as leaderboards. Users are strongly encouraged not to use their real names when entering a username.

Any stored data is used solely to improve the gameplay experience and is not shared with third parties.
